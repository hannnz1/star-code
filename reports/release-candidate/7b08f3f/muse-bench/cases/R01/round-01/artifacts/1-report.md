# Product comparison

## Summary table

| Product | Price | Project limit | Retention | Export |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Atlas | 120 USD | 40 projects | 7 days | CSV |
| Birch | 180 USD | 75 projects | 14 days | JSON |
| Cedar | 240 USD | 90 projects | 30 days | XML |

## Recommendation

**Best overall value: Birch.** It is more expensive than Atlas, but it roughly doubles the project limit and retention while offering a more flexible middle ground than Cedar. Cedar has the highest limits and longest retention, but at the highest price. Atlas is the cheapest, but also the most limited.
