# Product Comparison Report

Compared products: Atlas, Birch, Cedar.

## Values

| Product | Price | Project limit | Retention | Export |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Atlas | 120 USD | 40 projects | 7 days | CSV |
| Birch | 180 USD | 75 projects | 14 days | JSON |
| Cedar | 240 USD | 90 projects | 30 days | XML |

## Recommendation

**Birch** is the best overall balance if you want a mid-range option: it doubles Atlas’s retention, offers nearly twice the project limit, and costs less than Cedar while avoiding Atlas’s tighter limits. Choose **Atlas** if lowest price matters most, or **Cedar** if maximum retention and project capacity matter most.
