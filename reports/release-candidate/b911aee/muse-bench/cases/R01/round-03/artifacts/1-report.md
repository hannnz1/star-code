## Comparison

| Product | Price | Project limit | Retention | Export |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Atlas | 120 USD | 40 projects | 7 days | CSV |
| Birch | 180 USD | 75 projects | 14 days | JSON |
| Cedar | 240 USD | 90 projects | 30 days | XML |

## Recommendation

**Best overall value: Atlas.** It has the lowest price and still provides core capabilities, though its project limit and retention are the smallest of the three. Choose **Birch** if you need a better balance between cost and capacity. Choose **Cedar** only if the highest project limit and longest retention are worth the highest price.
