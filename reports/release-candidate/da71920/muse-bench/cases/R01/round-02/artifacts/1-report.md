## Recommendation

**Best overall value: Birch.** It is the middle-priced option, but it offers a much higher project limit than Atlas and a better retention window, while costing less than Cedar. If you need the longest retention or highest project cap, Cedar is stronger; if budget is the primary concern, Atlas is cheapest.
