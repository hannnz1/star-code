# Product comparison

## Summary table

| Product | Price | Project limit | Retention | Export |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Atlas | 120 USD | 40 projects | 7 days | CSV |
| Birch | 180 USD | 75 projects | 14 days | JSON |
| Cedar | 240 USD | 90 projects | 30 days | XML |

## Recommendation

**Cedar** is the best overall choice if you want the highest project limit and longest retention, though it is also the most expensive. **Atlas** is the lowest-cost option, while **Birch** sits in the middle with moderate pricing and capacity.
