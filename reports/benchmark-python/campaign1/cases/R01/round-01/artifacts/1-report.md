# Product Comparison Report

I compared Atlas, Birch, and Cedar across price, project limit, retention, and export format.

## Comparison table

| Product | Price | Project limit | Retention | Export |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Atlas | 120 USD | 40 projects | 7 days | CSV |
| Birch | 180 USD | 75 projects | 14 days | JSON |
| Cedar | 240 USD | 90 projects | 30 days | XML |

## Recommendation

**Cedar** is the best choice if you want the most generous limits and longest retention, and it also supports XML export. **Atlas** is the lowest-cost option, but it has the smallest limits and shortest retention. **Birch** is a middle ground on price and capability.
