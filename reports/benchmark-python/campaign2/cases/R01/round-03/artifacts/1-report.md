# Product comparison

I compared Atlas, Birch, and Cedar across price, project limit, retention, and export.

| Product | Price | Project limit | Retention | Export |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Atlas | 120 USD | 40 projects | 7 days | CSV |
| Birch | 180 USD | 75 projects | 14 days | JSON |
| Cedar | 240 USD | 90 projects | 30 days | XML |

## Notes
- Atlas is the lowest-priced option and has the shortest retention and smallest project limit.
- Birch is the middle option on price, project limit, and retention.
- Cedar is the most expensive but offers the highest project limit and longest retention.
- Export formats differ across all three products, so the best choice depends on which format you need.

## Recommendation
If cost is the main priority, choose **Atlas**. If you want the best overall balance of capacity and retention, choose **Birch**. If you need the largest project limit and longest retention regardless of price, choose **Cedar**.
